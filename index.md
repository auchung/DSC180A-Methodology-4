## Project Reflections

### 01 — Most Interesting Topic

**What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

One of the most interesting topics we've explored this quarter is how AI platforms influence the visibility of fertility clinics and the information patients receive about different providers. Our track examines whether RPSD appears in AI-generated responses, how prominently it is mentioned, and which competing clinics are recommended alongside it. I find it particularly interesting that AI platforms can produce different recommendations in response to similar questions, raising questions about their consistency and reliability. Since patients may rely on these responses when researching fertility treatment, understanding how clinics are represented is especially important.

### 02 — Potential Quarter 2 Investigation

**Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would be interested in investigating how different prompt engineering techniques influence AI-generated recommendations for fertility clinics. Specifically, we could explore how negations, changes in wording, and different ways of framing patient questions affect which clinics are recommended and how prominently they appear. I would also like to examine whether RPSD and its competitors are consistently recommended across different prompt variations or whether their visibility changes depending on how a question is presented. This would be able to help us better understand the sensitivity of AI-generated recommendations to prompt design.

### 03 — Improving Our Current Approach

**What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

One potential change I would make to our current approach is to improve how we distinguish between a clinic simply being mentioned and actually being recommended in AI-generated responses. Although our current methodology tracks clinic mentions and their positions, appearing earlier in a response does not necessarily mean that a clinic is being recommended more strongly. I think incorporating a more standardized evaluation process, including manually reviewing a sample of responses, could help us better identify explicit recommendations and validate our automated labels. This would make our comparisons between RPSD and competing clinics more reliable, particularly as we expand our analysis to include different prompt variations.

### 04 — Additional Techniques

**What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interested in exploring more systematic prompt engineering techniques alongside statistical analysis to better understand variations in AI-generated recommendations. For example, we could develop controlled prompt variations that change one element at a time, such as introducing a negation or emphasizing a specific patient concern, while keeping the rest of the question consistent. We could then use statistical testing to determine whether these changes produce meaningful differences in clinic visibility and recommendation positions across repeated trials. Additionally, I would like to explore information extraction techniques to compare which services and characteristics AI platforms highlight for RPSD and its competitors.
