**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

One of the most interesting topics we've explored is how AI-powered search platforms influence the visibility and reputation of fertility clinics. As patients increasingly use tools like ChatGPT and Gemini to research healthcare providers, the information presented by these systems can influence which clinics they consider. I find it particularly interesting that a clinic's visibility may depend not only on its actual services or reputation, but also on how AI models interpret and present information from different online sources.

**2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would be interested in investigating how different types of patient questions influence which fertility clinics are recommended by AI platforms. For example, we could compare broad discovery questions, clinic-specific questions, and direct comparisons between providers to determine whether certain clinics are consistently favored. By collecting responses across multiple prompts and platforms, we could analyze differences in mention frequency, recommendation ranking, and sentiment. The findings could help fertility clinics better understand their visibility in AI-generated search results.

**3. What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

One change I would make is to place greater emphasis on standardizing how we evaluate AI-generated responses. Since different models may provide recommendations in lists, paragraphs, or other formats, simply measuring the order in which clinics are mentioned may not accurately reflect how strongly they are recommended. I would develop a more consistent evaluation framework that distinguishes between mentions, explicit recommendations, and sentiment. This could improve the reliability of our results and make comparisons across platforms more meaningful.

**4. What other techniques would you be interested in using in your project?**

I would be interested in incorporating natural language processing and statistical analysis to better understand patterns in AI-generated recommendations. For example, we could use structured information extraction to identify clinic mentions and recommendation language, then apply statistical tests to determine whether differences across platforms or prompt categories are significant. I would also like to explore data visualization techniques to create an interactive dashboard showing how clinic visibility changes across different patient questions and AI models.
